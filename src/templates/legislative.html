Ordinance

A legislative measure that enacts a permanent rule or policy. Ordinances carry the force of law, create offices, levy taxes, or regulate conduct. They require three readings, publication, and the Mayor's approval (or passage by 2/3 override of a veto).

Resolution

A legislative measure that expresses the sentiment, intent, or opinion of the Sangguniang Bayan on a specific matter. Resolutions are used for internal housekeeping, requesting national action, or authorizing one-time transactions. They require only one reading and are not subject to publication.
