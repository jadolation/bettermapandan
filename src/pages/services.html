Permits, registrations, and frontline services offered by each municipal office, as published in Mapandan's Citizen's Charter.

Frontline Services

Who to see, and for what

Office Who It's For Services Offered

Sourced from Mapandan's official Citizen's Charter. Processing times and requirements are set by each office — contact them directly for current details.

Digital Government

National systems in local use

Mapandan participates in several national digital platforms that speed up identity verification and permit processing.

PhilSys

National ID system. Online Step 1 registration and local Step 2 biometric capture; PhilID/ePhilID accepted for municipal transactions.

CRS-ITP2

Civil Registry System digitizing document management and connecting the Municipal Civil Registrar to the central PSA database.

eGovPH Super App

Integrated via mapandan.gov.ph for local permit processing and digital IDs, managed by the DICT.

e-LGU Portal

Online local business permit and licensing portal for tax assessments, run by DICT/DILG.
