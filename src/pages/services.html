Citizen's Charter

Services Directory

Permits, registrations, and frontline services offered by each municipal office, as published in Mapandan's Citizen's Charter. Services are classified in compliance with Republic Act No. 11032 (Ease of Doing Business Act).

Digital Government

National systems in local use

Mapandan participates in several national digital platforms that speed up identity verification and permit processing.

PhilSys

National ID system. Online Step 1 registration and local Step 2 biometric capture; PhilID/ePhilID accepted for municipal transactions.

CRS-ITP2

Civil Registry System digitizing document management and connecting the Municipal Civil Registrar to the central PSA database.

eGovPH Super App

Integrated via mapandan.gov.ph for local permit processing and digital IDs, managed by the DICT.

e-LGU Portal

Online local business permit and licensing portal for tax assessments, run by DICT/DILG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Citizen service guide

How can I request public documents?

Residents may request official records under Executive Order No. 2 (Freedom of Information) by submitting a written request to the Mayor's Office or the designated department custodian. Standardized records such as birth certificates or tax declarations can be requested directly from the Municipal Civil Registrar or Municipal Assessor.

How do I submit feedback or complaints?

Public feedback or complaints regarding frontline services can be directed to the Human Resource Management Office (HRMO) or the Mayor's Office. Citizens can also use national channels including Hotline 8888 and local feedback drop boxes inside the Municipal Hall lobby.

How can businesses access digital permitting?

Commercial entities can access the e-LGU portal through the official municipal website (mapandan.gov.ph) or use the national eGovPH Super App to initiate business registration and renewal workflows.
