Description

Issuance of certified copies of birth, marriage, death, and other civil registry documents from the Municipal Civil Registrar. The MCR verifies records through CRIS/Registry Book and prepares the certificate. Fee: ₱115 per copy. For PSA endorsement: additional ₱200. Various MCR forms are available depending on document availability and status.

Requirements

  • MCR Form 1A (Birth authorization) / 1B (Birth not available) / 1C (Birth destroyed)
  • MCR Form 3A (Marriage) / 3B (Marriage not available) / 3C (Marriage destroyed)
  • MCR Form 2A (Death not available) / 3C (Death destroyed)

Procedure

  1. Request certification from MCR. Staff verifies through CRIS/Registry Book and prepares certificate (15 min).
  2. MCR certifies the facts stated as to correctness.
  3. Proceed to Treasury Office for payment of ₱115 per copy.
  4. Present OR to MCR Office. Documents released.
  5. For PSA endorsement: MCR verifies and prepares endorsement (₱200, 10 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Civil Registrar (MCR)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: ~18 minutes

Fee: ₱115 per copy; ₱200 for PSA endorsement

Where to Apply: Municipal Civil Registrar's Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
