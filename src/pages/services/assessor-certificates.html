Description

The Municipal Assessor's Office issues various certificates related to real property beyond the standard Tax Declaration copies. These include clearance certificates, non-declaration certificates, and other property-related certifications needed for legal, financial, and administrative transactions. The fee is ₱100 per certificate.

Requirements

  • Written request specifying the type of certificate needed
  • Valid Government-issued ID
  • Property details (lot number, block, street, barangay)

Procedure

  1. Submit a written request with property details and valid ID to the Assessor's Office.
  2. Staff receives and reviews the request (3 min).
  3. Staff verifies the information in the records (7 min).
  4. Staff prepares and issues the certificate (3 min).
  5. Pay ₱100 at the Treasury Office and claim the certificate (2 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Assessor's Office (MASSO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 15 minutes

Fee: ₱100

Where to Apply: Municipal Assessor's Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
