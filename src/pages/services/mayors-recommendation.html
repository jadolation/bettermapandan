Description

The Office of the Mayor issues recommendation letters for residents of Mapandan who are applying for employment, scholarship, or other opportunities requiring a character reference from the Local Chief Executive. The staff reviews the application, prepares the recommendation letter, and submits it to the Mayor for signature.

Requirements

  • Letter of Request stating the purpose
  • Valid Government-issued ID
  • Barangay Certification of Residency
  • Resume or CV (for employment)

Procedure

  1. Submit letter of request, valid ID, and barangay certification to the Mayor's Office.
  2. Staff reviews and verifies the documents (5 min).
  3. Staff prepares the recommendation letter for the Mayor's signature (5 min).
  4. Receive the signed recommendation letter (5 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Office of the Municipal Mayor

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 15 minutes

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: Office of the Municipal Mayor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
