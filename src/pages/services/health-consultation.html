Description

The Municipal Health Office (MHO) / Rural Health Unit (RHU) provides free health consultation services to all residents of Mapandan. This includes general medical consultations, health advice, and referrals to higher-level health facilities when necessary. Walk-in patients are served on a first-come, first-served basis.

Requirements

  • Valid ID (government issued, employment, school, barangay certification)

Procedure

  1. Register in Electronic Medical Record (EMR). Midwife registers new patients and encodes data.
  2. Classify patient: PhilHealth member (attach ID number) or non-PhilHealth member.
  3. Record consultation date, vital signs, and chief complaint.
  4. Occupy seats in waiting area and wait for name to be called.
  5. MHO check-up, assess, and treat the patient. Schedule follow-up check-up.
  6. Give available medicines to patient and encode.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Health Office (MHO / RHU)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 25 minutes per client

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: Rural Health Unit, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1874
