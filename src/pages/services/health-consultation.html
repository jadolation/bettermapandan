Health Consultation

Free health consultation services for Mapandan residents at the Rural Health Unit. Walk-in services available for all residents.

Home › Services › Health Consultation

Description

The Municipal Health Office (MHO) / Rural Health Unit (RHU) provides free health consultation services to all residents of Mapandan. This includes general medical consultations, health advice, and referrals to higher-level health facilities when necessary. Walk-in patients are served on a first-come, first-served basis.

Requirements

  • None — open to all residents

Procedure

  1. Visit the Rural Health Unit (RHU) in Poblacion during office hours.
  2. Register at the reception counter and provide your basic information.
  3. Wait for your name to be called for consultation.
  4. Consult with the attending physician or health officer.
  5. Receive prescription or referrals, if applicable.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Health Office (MHO / RHU)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: Walk-in (same day)

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: Rural Health Unit, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1874
