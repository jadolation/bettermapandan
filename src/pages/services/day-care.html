Description

The MSWDO operates a Day Care Service Program for pre-schoolers in the barangay. The program provides early childhood education through regular sessions conducted by Child Development Workers at Day Care Centers. Parents register their children and attend regular sessions and parents' meetings. The program also includes annual events such as Nutrition Month Celebration, Children's Congress, and Family Day.

Requirements

  • Child must be of pre-school age
  • Registration at the Day Care Center

Procedure

  1. Register your child at the Day Care Center in your barangay.
  2. Fill out the registration forms. Staff will assess the child's birth certificate.
  3. Send or accompany your child to the Day Care Center for regular sessions (2–3 hours).
  4. Attend Day Care parents' meetings as scheduled.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 2–5 minutes (registration); ongoing sessions

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: Day Care Center in your barangay

Contact: (075) 632-1751
