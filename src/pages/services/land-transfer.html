Description

Transfer of land ownership is the legal process of transferring property rights from one owner to another. This process involves coordination between the Municipal Assessor’s Office (MASSO) and the Municipal Treasury Office (MTO) to update property records and ensure proper tax assessment. The transfer may be due to sale, donation, inheritance, or other legal means.

Requirements

  • Deed of sale or deed of donation (depending on transfer type)
  • Tax clearance from the Municipal Treasurer’s Office
  • Valid government-issued ID of the new owner
  • Original tax declaration
  • Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) certificate of registration

Procedure

  1. Secure a tax clearance from the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.
  2. Obtain the BIR certificate of registration for the transfer.
  3. Submit all required documents to the Municipal Assessor’s Office (MASSO).
  4. Pay the assessor’s fee based on the property value.
  5. Wait for processing and verification of documents.
  6. Receive the updated tax declaration under the new owner’s name.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Assessor’s Office (MASSO) + Municipal Treasury Office (MTO)

Classification: Complex

Processing Time: 15–30 working days

Fee: Varies (assessor’s fee based on property value)

Where to Apply: Municipal Assessor’s Office, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164
