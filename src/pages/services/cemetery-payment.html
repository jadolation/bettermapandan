Description

Payment for cemetery lot rental and purchase of cemetery tombs at the Mapandan cemetery. Cemetery lot rental is ₱1,500 every ten years. Cemetery tombs are available at ₱10,000, ₱15,000, and ₱20,000. A death certificate is required for tomb purchase.

Requirements

  • Death Certificate (from Municipal Civil Registry)

Procedure

  1. Go to Counter 8 and inquire for available tomb or lot rental.
  2. Staff checks availability (20 min).
  3. Pay the required fees (3 min).
  4. Receive Official Receipt. Staff records in Cemetery log book.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasurer's Office

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 25 minutes

Fee: ₱1,500/10yr (lot rental); ₱10,000–₱20,000 (tombs)

Where to Apply: Counter 8, Municipal Treasury Office, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831

Source: Mapandan Citizen's Charter
