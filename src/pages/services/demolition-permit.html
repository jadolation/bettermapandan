Description

A demolition permit is required before any demolition of a building or structure in Mapandan. The Municipal Engineer conducts initial assessment of the application, site inspection, and computation of fees. The permit is issued after payment and verification of all requirements.

Requirements

  • Application for demolition permit
  • Approved demolition plans (if applicable)
  • Proof of ownership or authorization from owner

Procedure

  1. Report to the Engineering Office for assessment of your application (15 min).
  2. Engineering staff conducts site inspection (1 hour).
  3. Staff computes the required assessment fee (15 min).
  4. Pay the necessary fees at the Treasury Office and obtain official receipt (10 min).
  5. Present OR to Engineering Staff. Staff prepares the demolition permit (15 min).
  6. Receive the demolition permit (5 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Engineer's Office

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: ~2 hours

Fee: Varies depending on inspection results

Where to Apply: Municipal Engineer's Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
