Description

A Professional Tax Receipt is required for all professionals exercising their profession in the Municipality of Mapandan. The fee is ₱300 for professionals (to be remitted to the Provincial Treasury Office) and ₱100 for non-professionals. Professionals must present their PRC ID, while non-professionals must present their Mayor's Permit.

Requirements

  • Professional ID from PRC (for professionals)
  • Mayor's Permit from Mayor's Office (for non-professionals)

Procedure

  1. Go to Counters 1 and 2 and request for Professional Tax Receipt.
  2. Pay the required fee: ₱300 for professionals or ₱100 for non-professionals.
  3. Receive official receipt.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasurer's Office

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 15 minutes

Fee: ₱300 (professionals) / ₱100 (non-professionals)

Where to Apply: Counters 1-2, Municipal Treasury Office, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
