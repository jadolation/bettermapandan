Business Permitting & Licensing

New Business Permit

Secure your new business permit to operate commercially in the Municipality of Mapandan.

Home › Services › New Business Permit

Description

A business permit is required for all individuals or entities intending to operate a commercial business within the Municipality of Mapandan. This permit is issued after compliance with documentary and safety requirements and upon payment of applicable fees.

Requirements

  • DTI or SEC registration certificate
  • Barangay clearance from the barangay where the business is located
  • Fire safety inspection certificate
  • Occupancy permit (if renting a commercial space)
  • Sketch of business location
  • Valid government-issued ID of the business owner or authorized representative

Procedure

  1. Secure a barangay clearance from the barangay where the business will be located.
  2. Gather all required documents and submit them to the Municipal Treasurer’s Office (MTO).
  3. Pay the corresponding business permit fees based on your business category.
  4. Receive your official business permit upon successful processing.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasurer’s Office (MTO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 3–5 working days

Fee: Based on business category (schedule available at MTO)

Where to Apply: 1st Floor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164
