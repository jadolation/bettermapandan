Description

The MSWDO provides care and protection services for Children in Conflict with the Law (CICL) and children under difficult circumstances. Services include surveillance, rescue and recovery, counselling, referral to PNP, temporary shelter, and custody care. The MSWDO coordinates with the PNP, hospitals, and Prosecutor's Office as necessary.

Requirements

  • Birth Certificate (PSA)
  • Medical Certificate / Medico-legal report
  • Police Blotter Report

Procedure

  1. Register at the MSWD Office. Staff will refer the client for intake.
  2. Discuss the problem and provide relevant information. Staff interviews and gathers data.
  3. Staff refers the client to other agencies (PNP, Hospital, Prosecutor's Office) if necessary.
  4. Staff prepares draft of Social Case Study Report and follows up with home visits and counseling.
  5. Present results. MSWDO prepares and submits Social Case Study Report.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 1 hour or more

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: MSWDO Office, Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1751
