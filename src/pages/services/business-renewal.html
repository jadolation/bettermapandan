Description

Businesses operating within Mapandan are required to renew their business permits annually. Renewal ensures that your business remains compliant with local regulations and avoids penalties for late filing.

Requirements

  • Previous year’s business permit
  • Updated DTI or SEC registration certificate
  • Latest Barangay clearance
  • Fire safety inspection certificate
  • Valid government-issued ID of the business owner

Procedure

  1. Secure an updated barangay clearance from your barangay.
  2. Submit all required documents to the Municipal Treasurer’s Office (MTO).
  3. Pay the renewal fee based on your business category.
  4. Receive your renewed business permit.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasurer’s Office (MTO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 1–3 working days

Fee: Based on business category (schedule available at MTO)

Where to Apply: 1st Floor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164
