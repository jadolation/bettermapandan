Description

The Population Office provides pre-marriage orientation and counseling to couples planning to get married as required before obtaining a marriage license. The session covers family planning methods, reproductive health, responsible parenthood, and RA 9255. A Certificate of Pre-Marriage Counseling is issued upon completion, which is a prerequisite for marriage license application.

Requirements

  • Valid Government-issued ID of both parties
  • Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) from Treasury Office

Procedure

  1. Visit the Population Office with your partner and present valid IDs.
  2. Staff records the couple's information and schedules the session.
  3. Attend the pre-marriage orientation and counseling session.
  4. Receive the Certificate of Pre-Marriage Counseling upon completion.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Population Office

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 15 minutes (session duration varies)

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: Municipal Population Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
