Description

The Municipal Assessor's Office issues a Landholdings Certificate showing all real properties registered under a person's name within the Municipality of Mapandan. This certificate is used for tax compliance verification, legal transactions, and property portfolio documentation. The client submits a written request, and the Assessor's Office compiles the information from the property records.

Requirements

  • Written request stating the name of the property owner
  • Valid Government-issued ID

Procedure

  1. Submit a written request with the property owner's name and valid ID to the Assessor's Office.
  2. Staff receives and verifies the request (5 min).
  3. Staff searches the records for all properties under the name (5 min).
  4. Staff compiles and prepares the Landholdings Certificate (3 min).
  5. Pay ₱100 at the Treasury Office and claim the certificate (2 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Assessor's Office (MASSO)

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 15 minutes

Fee: ₱100

Where to Apply: Municipal Assessor's Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
