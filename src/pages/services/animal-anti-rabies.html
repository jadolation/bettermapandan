Description

The Municipal Agriculturist provides consultation, treatment, and anti-rabies vaccination for pets and animals. The Livestock Inspector obtains the animal's medical history, conducts a physical examination, and vaccinates the animal. This service helps prevent the spread of rabies in the community.

Requirements

  • Bring the animal to the Municipal Agriculture Office

Procedure

  1. Bring the animal to the Municipal Agriculture Office.
  2. Livestock Inspector obtains the animal's medical history and conducts physical examination (30 min).
  3. Livestock Inspector vaccinates the animal (5 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Agriculturist Office (MAO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 35 minutes

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: Municipal Agriculture Office, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1873
