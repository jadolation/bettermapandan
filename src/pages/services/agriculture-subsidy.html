Description

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) provides various government-subsidized agricultural programs to support local farmers and fisherfolk in Mapandan. These programs include subsidies for seeds, fertilizers, farm equipment, and technical assistance to improve agricultural productivity and livelihood. Availability of subsidies depends on national and provincial funding allocations.

Requirements

  • Farmer registration with the Municipal Agriculture Office
  • Barangay certification of residency and farming activity
  • Farm location and land area information

Procedure

  1. Register with the Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) as a farmer or fisherfolk.
  2. Secure a barangay certification confirming your farming activity and residency.
  3. Submit your registration and requirements to the MAO.
  4. Wait for availability announcement of agricultural subsidies or programs.
  5. Receive agricultural assistance or subsidies upon program approval.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: Varies (depends on availability)

Fee: Free (government-subsidized)

Where to Apply: Municipal Agriculture Office, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1873
