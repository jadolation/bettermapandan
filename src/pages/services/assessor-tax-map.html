Description

The Municipal Assessor's Office issues tax maps showing the location, boundaries, dimensions, and lot details of real properties within Mapandan. Tax maps are required for property transactions, land use planning, and other legal purposes. The client submits a written request with the lot details, and the Assessor's Office prepares the tax map from their records.

Requirements

  • Written request specifying the lot number, block, street, and barangay
  • Valid Government-issued ID

Procedure

  1. Submit a written request with lot details and valid ID to the Assessor's Office.
  2. Staff receives and verifies the request (3 min).
  3. Staff locates the tax map in the records (7 min).
  4. Staff prepares and issues the tax map (3 min).
  5. Pay ₱100 at the Treasury Office and claim the tax map (2 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Assessor's Office (MASSO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 15 minutes

Fee: ₱100

Where to Apply: Municipal Assessor's Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
