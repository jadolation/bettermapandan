Description

The RHU implements the Nutrition Program for malnourished children (0–59 months and older) and the National Immunization Program (NIP) for children against vaccine-preventable diseases. The nutrition program includes assessment, deworming, feeding with nutrient-dense food, and monthly monitoring. The immunization program administers age-appropriate vaccines after reviewing the child's records and checking vital signs.

Requirements

  • Birth Certificate (Municipal Civil Registry)
  • Medical Records, Vaccination Records (from hospital of birth or pediatrician)

Procedure

  1. For malnourished children (0–59 months): Barangay Nutrition Scholar assesses weight and height.
  2. Physician refers to RHU for deworming and medical intervention.
  3. Child is fed with nutrient-dense food (high carbohydrate and protein).
  4. Monthly monitoring of weight, height, and alertness.
  5. For immunization: BHW interviews child's information and reviews records.
  6. Midwife checks vital signs (weight and temperature).
  7. Midwife administers age-appropriate vaccine.
  8. Midwife advises parent on child care and expected outcome.
  9. Schedule next vaccination.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Rural Health Unit (RHU)

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 50 minutes per client

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: Rural Health Unit, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1874
