Description

A sanitary permit is required for all businesses that handle food, beverages, or other consumable products to ensure compliance with health and sanitation standards. The permit is issued by the Municipal Health Office (MHO) after an inspection of the business premises to verify that health and safety regulations are met.

Requirements

  • Health certificate from the MHO
  • Food handler’s certificate (if applicable for food businesses)
  • Business permit from the Municipal Treasurer’s Office
  • Inspection of premises by MHO health officer

Procedure

  1. Secure a health certificate and food handler’s certificate (if applicable) from the MHO.
  2. Request an inspection of your business premises by a health officer.
  3. Pay the corresponding sanitary permit fee based on your business type.
  4. Receive your sanitary permit after successful inspection and payment.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Health Office (MHO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 3–5 working days

Fee: Based on business type

Where to Apply: Municipal Health Office, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1874
