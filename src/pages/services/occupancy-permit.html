Description

An occupancy permit certifies that a building or structure has been completed and inspected in accordance with the approved plans and the National Building Code. It is required before any building may be legally occupied or used.

Requirements

  • Approved building permit
  • As-built plans (reflecting the actual construction)
  • Inspection report from the Municipal Engineering Office
  • Fire safety inspection certificate from the Bureau of Fire Protection

Procedure

  1. Request a final inspection from the Municipal Engineering Office.
  2. Submit the as-built plans and inspection report.
  3. Obtain a fire safety inspection certificate from the BFP.
  4. Submit all documents to the MEO for review.
  5. Receive the occupancy permit upon approval.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Engineering Office (MEO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 3–5 working days

Fee: As scheduled by the MEO

Where to Apply: Municipal Engineering Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion
