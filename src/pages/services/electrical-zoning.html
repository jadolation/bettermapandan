Description

A zoning clearance is required for electrical permits. The MPDC/Zoning Office evaluates the application, conducts land use assessment, and issues the Zoning Compliance Certificate for electrical installations. The process includes site evaluation/inspection, computation of fees, and approval by the Zoning Officer.

Requirements

  • Application for Zoning Permit (from MPDC)
  • Accomplished Individual Assessment for Application of Temporary Housing Unit Form
  • Tax Declaration of lot (from Assessor Office)
  • Current Tax receipts (from Treasury Office)
  • Affidavit of consent of lot owner
  • Site development sketch indicating the ROW
  • Affidavit of consent for Right of Way (from lot owner)
  • Certification from Barangay Captain

Procedure

  1. Inquire at the MPDC office and secure the application form.
  2. MPDC staff conducts interview and provides forms.
  3. Accomplish forms and prepare all required documents.
  4. MPDC checks and evaluates forms and required documents.
  5. Request for site evaluation/inspection. Land use assessment conducted.
  6. Go to Zoning office for computation of fees and charges.
  7. Pay fees at the Treasury Office.
  8. Present receipts to MPDC. Submit one photocopy of all forms and documents.
  9. MPDC prepares and submits documents. Zoning Officer approves application.
  10. Client signs logbook.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDC) / Zoning Office

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: ~1.5 hours

Fee: ₱330 (Zoning fee ₱200 + Affidavit ₱100 + Documentary Stamp ₱30)

Where to Apply: MPDC / Zoning Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
