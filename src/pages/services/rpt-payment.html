Description

Real property tax (RPT) is an annual tax on the assessed value of land, buildings, and improvements owned by individuals or entities within the Municipality of Mapandan. Payment of RPT is required under the Local Government Code (Republic Act No. 7160) and Provincial Ordinance No. 253-2020. The tax is used to fund local services, infrastructure, and public welfare programs.

Requirements

  • Tax declaration from the Municipal Assessor’s Office
  • Previous year’s official receipt
  • Valid government-issued ID

Procedure

  1. Present your tax declaration and previous year’s official receipt at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.
  2. Verify the current assessed value of your property with the assessor.
  3. Pay the corresponding real property tax at the treasury counter.
  4. Receive your official receipt as proof of payment.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasury Office (MTO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: Same day

Fee: Based on assessed value (1% annual for basic, additional for SEF)

Where to Apply: 1st Floor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164

Source: Mapandan Citizen’s Charter / Provincial Ordinance No. 253-2020
