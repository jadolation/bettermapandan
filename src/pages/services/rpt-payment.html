Description

Real property tax (RPT) is an annual tax on the assessed value of land, buildings, and improvements within Mapandan. Computation: Basic Tax = 1% of Assessed Value + Special Education Tax (SEF) = 1% of Assessed Value. Discounts: 10% for prompt payment, 20% for advance payment. Interest on unpaid real property tax: 2% per month but not to exceed 72%. Payment is processed at Counters 4-9 of the Treasury Office.

Requirements

  • Real Property Tax bill (from Assessor's Office)
  • Previous Tax receipt (if available)

Procedure

  1. Go to Counters 4 to 9 and request computation of real property tax.
  2. Staff computes the real property tax due (20 min).
  3. Pay the required fees (5 min).
  4. Receive the official receipt. Staff records in general collection system (5 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasury Office (MTO)

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 30 minutes (20 min computation + 5 min payment + 5 min recording)

Fee: Basic Tax (1% of AV) + SEF (1% of AV); 10% prompt payment discount; 20% advance payment discount

Where to Apply: 1st Floor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164

Source: Mapandan Citizen’s Charter / Provincial Ordinance No. 253-2020

Last Updated: August 2025
