Description

The MSWDO issues certifications for students who wish to avail of scholarship programs, tuition fee discounts, or educational assistance from schools, universities, and government agencies. The certification verifies the student's residency and financial status, which is required by most scholarship-granting institutions.

Requirements

  • Request letter stating the purpose
  • Valid ID (school ID or government-issued)

Procedure

  1. Request the list of requirements from MSWDO staff.
  2. Staff will explain the requirements and process.
  3. MSWDO prepares the certification for educational assistance, school fees discount, or scholarship.
  4. Receive the issued certification.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 5–10 minutes

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: MSWDO Office, Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1751
