Zoning Clearance

Obtain a zoning clearance before constructing, renovating, or changing the use of a property in Mapandan.

Home › Services › Zoning Clearance

Description

A zoning clearance certifies that the intended use or construction on a property complies with the municipality’s land use plan and zoning regulations. It is a prerequisite for obtaining a building permit or for any change in land use classification.

Requirements

  • Certificate of land title or ownership documents (e.g., Tax Declaration, Deed of Sale)
  • Site development plan or proposed layout
  • Sketch of property location (showing the lot in relation to nearby streets/landmarks)

Procedure

  1. Visit the Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDC) or Zoning Office.
  2. Submit the required documents along with the zoning clearance application form.
  3. Pay the applicable fee at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.
  4. Receive the zoning clearance after verification of land use compliance.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDC) / Zoning Office

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 3–5 working days

Fee: As scheduled by the MPDC

Where to Apply: MPDC / Zoning Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164
