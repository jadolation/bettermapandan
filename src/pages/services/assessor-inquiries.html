Description

The Municipal Assessor's Office provides information and verification services regarding real property records, assessed values, tax declarations, and other property-related inquiries. The client may inquire about the status of their property, verify assessed values, check for outstanding obligations, or request general information about property taxation. The Assessor's Office staff provides the information from their records.

Requirements

  • Valid Government-issued ID
  • Property details (lot number, block, street, barangay) if inquiring about a specific property

Procedure

  1. Visit the Assessor's Office and present your valid ID.
  2. State your inquiry or verification request to the staff.
  3. Staff looks up the information in the records (10 min).
  4. Staff provides the information or prints the verification document (3 min).
  5. Pay ₱100 at the Treasury Office if a printed document is required (2 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Assessor's Office (MASSO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 15 minutes

Fee: ₱100

Where to Apply: Municipal Assessor's Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
