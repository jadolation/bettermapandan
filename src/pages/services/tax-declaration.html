Tax Declaration Copy

Request a copy of your tax declaration from the Municipal Assessor's Office in Mapandan. Learn about fees, requirements, and processing time.

Home › Services › Tax Declaration Copy

Description

A tax declaration is an official document that shows the assessed value of your real property for taxation purposes. Property owners may request certified copies of their tax declaration for various legal, financial, and administrative transactions, including property sales, loans, and insurance applications.

Requirements

  • Valid government-issued ID
  • Written request stating the purpose of the request
  • Payment of ₱50 per copy

Procedure

  1. Submit a written request to the Municipal Assessor’s Office (MASSO) stating the purpose of your request.
  2. Present your valid government-issued ID for verification.
  3. Pay ₱50 per copy at the designated payment counter.
  4. Receive your certified tax declaration copy after processing.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Assessor’s Office (MASSO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 1–3 working days

Fee: ₱50 per copy

Where to Apply: Municipal Assessor’s Office, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164
