Description

A marriage license is a legal requirement before a marriage can be solemnized in the Philippines. The Municipal Civil Registrar (MCR) of Mapandan processes marriage license applications, which include a required publication period. The license is valid for 120 days from the date of issuance.

Requirements

  • Birth certificates (PSA-authenticated) of both contracting parties
  • Valid government-issued IDs of both parties
  • Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR) for both parties
  • Parental consent or advice (if either party is under 25 years old)
  • Certificate of Marriage Counseling (from a government-accredited counselor)
  • Community Tax Certificate (cedula) for both parties
  • Two (2) ID photos of each party

Procedure

  1. Both parties appear at the MCR and submit all required documents.
  2. Fill out the marriage license application form.
  3. Pay the applicable fees at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.
  4. Wait for the 10-day publication period.
  5. After publication, claim the marriage license from the MCR.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Civil Registrar (MCR)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 10–15 working days (including 10-day publication period)

Fee: As scheduled by the MCR

Where to Apply: Municipal Civil Registrar’s Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164
