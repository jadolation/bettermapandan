Description

The RHU provides anti-rabies treatment for animal bite patients through the Animal Bite Treatment Center (ABTC). The service includes wound assessment, counselling, prescription of rabies vaccine or immunoglobulin, and post-treatment instructions. The first dose is free for 4P's members and Mapandan residents. Subsequent doses cost ₱350 each.

Requirements

  • Rabies exposure case (bite or scratch)

Procedure

  1. Register in the log book. Midwife classifies patient (new/old, PhilHealth member, emergency/senior citizen priority).
  2. Record patient's data, vital signs, and bite/scratch details.
  3. MHO performs wound/scratch assessment and provides counselling.
  4. MHO prescribes rabies vaccine or immunoglobulin (as applicable).
  5. ABTC Nurse administers anti-rabies vaccine and/or immunoglobulin.
  6. ABTC Nurse provides post-treatment instructions and follow-up schedule.
  7. Pharmacy Assistant dispenses vaccines or medicine.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Rural Health Unit (RHU)

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 37 minutes per client

Fee: Free (1st dose for Mapandanians and 4P's members); ₱350 for subsequent doses

Where to Apply: Rural Health Unit (ABTC), Poblacion

Contact: (075) 632-1874
