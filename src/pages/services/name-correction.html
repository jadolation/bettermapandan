Description

Republic Act No. 9048 authorizes the city or municipal civil registrar to correct clerical or typographical errors in civil registry entries without the need for a judicial order. This covers corrections in first names, nicknames, or minor spelling errors. This process does not apply to changes in gender, date of birth, or other substantive entries.

Requirements

  • PSA-authenticated birth certificate (original and photocopy)
  • Valid government-issued ID of the petitioner
  • Supporting documents that prove the correct name (e.g., school records, baptismal certificate, IDs)
  • Notarized affidavit of the petitioner explaining the error
  • Publication of the correction in a local newspaper (for the required period)

Procedure

  1. Visit the Municipal Civil Registrar’s Office and submit all required documents.
  2. File the petition for correction of clerical error.
  3. Pay the required fees at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.
  4. Wait for the publication period to be completed.
  5. After publication and verification, claim the corrected birth certificate from the MCR.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Civil Registrar (MCR)

Classification: Complex

Processing Time: 15–30 working days (including publication period)

Fee: As scheduled by the MCR (includes publication cost)

Where to Apply: Municipal Civil Registrar’s Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion
