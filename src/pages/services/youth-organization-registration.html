Description

The Local Youth Development Office (LYDO) administers the Youth Organization Registration Program (YORP) as mandated by the SK Reform Act (RA 11769). Registration provides official recognition of the youth organization, access to LGU support and funding opportunities, and participation in municipal, provincial, and national youth programs and activities. The LYDO assesses the application, verifies membership composition, and issues the Certificate of Registration.

Requirements

  • Letter of Intent to Register from the organization
  • Constitution and By-Laws of the organization
  • List of Officers and Members with ages (must be 15–30 years old)
  • Minutes of the organization's founding meeting
  • Endorsement from the Barangay Captain (if barangay-level organization)

Procedure

  1. Submit the letter of intent and all required documents to the LYDO Office.
  2. LYDO staff receives and reviews the application (3 min).
  3. LYDO verifies the membership composition and constitution (3 min).
  4. LYDO prepares the Certificate of Registration (2 min).
  5. Receive the Certificate of Registration upon approval (2 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Local Youth Development Office (LYDO)

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 10 minutes (processing); registration valid upon approval

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: LYDO Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
