Description

Property owners who have not declared their real property for taxation purposes may voluntarily declare it with the Municipal Assessor's Office. The Assessor appraises the land and any improvements, and issues a new Tax Declaration. This process brings the property into the tax rolls and avoids penalties for non-declaration. The fee covers the appraisal and declaration process.

Requirements

  • Valid Government-issued ID of the property owner
  • Proof of ownership (Deed of Sale, Deed of Donation, Tax-Exempt Certificate, or Inheritance documents)
  • Blueprints or sketches of the property (if available)
  • Barangay Certification of ownership

Procedure

  1. Visit the Assessor's Office and submit all required documents.
  2. Staff receives and reviews the application (5 min).
  3. Assessor conducts appraisal of the land and improvements (15 min).
  4. Staff prepares the Tax Declaration (5 min).
  5. Pay ₱700 at the Treasury Office and claim the Tax Declaration (5 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Assessor's Office (MASSO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 30 minutes

Fee: ₱700

Where to Apply: Municipal Assessor's Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
