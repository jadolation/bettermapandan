Description

The Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO) receives and processes complaints related to environmental violations, pollution, illegal dumping, and other environmental concerns in Mapandan. The client submits a complaint with supporting evidence, and the MENRO investigates, verifies the complaint, and takes appropriate enforcement action. The MENRO coordinates with the PNP, Barangay officials, and other agencies as needed.

Requirements

  • Written complaint describing the environmental violation
  • Evidence of violation (photos, videos, or other documentation if available)
  • Valid Government-issued ID of the complainant

Procedure

  1. Submit the written complaint with evidence and valid ID to the MENRO Office.
  2. MENRO staff receives and evaluates the complaint (5 min).
  3. MENRO verifies the complaint through site inspection or investigation (varies).
  4. MENRO takes appropriate enforcement action and coordinates with concerned agencies.
  5. Follow up with MENRO for the status of the complaint.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 15 minutes (initial processing); investigation varies

Fee: Free

Where to Apply: MENRO Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831
