Description

The Office of the Mayor issues various clearances and certificates to residents and businesses of Mapandan. The staff receives the request, verifies the applicant's records, prepares the document, and submits it to the Mayor for signature. The client pays the required fee at the Treasury Office before claiming the document.

Requirements

  • Letter of Request
  • Valid Government-issued ID
  • Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) from Treasury Office

Procedure

  1. Submit letter of request and valid ID to the Mayor's Office.
  2. Staff receives and reviews the request (5 min).
  3. Pay ₱130 at the Treasury Office and obtain official receipt.
  4. Present OR to the Mayor's Office. Staff prepares the document for the Mayor's signature (5 min).
  5. Receive the signed clearance or certificate (5 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Office of the Municipal Mayor

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 15 minutes

Fee: ₱130

Where to Apply: Office of the Municipal Mayor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831

Source: Mapandan Citizen's Charter
