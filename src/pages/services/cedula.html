Description

The Community Tax Certificate, commonly known as a cedula, is a requirement for residents of Mapandan engaged in certain transactions with government agencies. It serves as proof of community tax payment and is required for various legal, business, and personal transactions. The cedula is issued under the provisions of the Local Government Code (Republic Act No. 7160).

Requirements

  • Valid government-issued ID
  • Payment of community tax

Procedure

  1. Present your valid ID at the Municipal Treasurer’s Office.
  2. Pay the basic community tax of ₱500.
  3. If applicable, pay an additional ₱1 per ₱1,000 of gross income earned during the preceding year.
  4. Receive your community tax certificate (cedula).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasury Office (MTO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: Same day

Fee: ₱500 (basic community tax) + ₱1 per ₱1,000 of income (if applicable)

Where to Apply: 1st Floor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164

Source: Mapandan Citizen’s Charter
