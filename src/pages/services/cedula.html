Description

The Community Tax Certificate (cedula) is computed as follows: Individual — ₱5.00 basic + ₱1.00 for every ₱1,000 gross income from business, profession, or property, but in no case shall exceed ₱5,000.00. Juridical — ₱500.00 basic + ₱2.00 for every ₱5,000 gross income from business or worth of real property, but in no case shall exceed ₱10,000.00. Interest of 24% per annum shall be imposed on individual or juridical securing their CTC from March 1 up to the end of the year.

Requirements

  • Old Community Tax Certificate (if available)
  • Proof of income (if available)

Procedure

  1. Go to Counters 1 and 2 and fill up the basic data form (15 min).
  2. Pay the required fees (3 min).
  3. Receive community tax certificate. Staff records in general collection system (2 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasury Office (MTO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 20 minutes (15 min form filling + 3 min payment + 2 min recording)

Fee: Individual: ₱5 + ₱1/₱1k income (max ₱5,000); Juridical: ₱500 + ₱2/₱5k income (max ₱10,000); 24% interest from March 1

Where to Apply: 1st Floor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164
