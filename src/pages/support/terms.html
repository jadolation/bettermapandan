Purpose

Better Mapandan is an independent civic transparency portal that compiles publicly available government information for the Municipality of Mapandan, Pangasinan. It is not an official government website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Local Government Unit of Mapandan or any government agency.

Information is compiled from publicly available sources and is subject to change. Some data is clearly marked as "Placeholder" when the exact figure is not yet available from official sources.

For official government information, always contact the Municipality of Mapandan directly or visit mapandan.gov.ph.
