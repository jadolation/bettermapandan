Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about government services and civic information in Mapandan.

General

About Better Mapandan

Is this an official government website?

No. Better Mapandan is an independent civic transparency portal built and maintained by volunteers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Municipality of Mapandan or any government agency. For official information, visit mapandan.gov.ph.

How is this project funded?

Better Mapandan is entirely volunteer-run and uses no government funding. The cost to the people of Mapandan is ₱0. Hosting and domain costs are covered by the BetterGov.ph initiative.

Where does the data come from?

All data is compiled from publicly available government sources: COA audit reports, PSA census data, DILG disclosures, BLGF treasury data, provincial issuances, and municipal announcements. Every source is attributed on the page where the data appears.

How do I report incorrect information?

Visit our Report Incorrect Information page. Provide the page URL, the specific error, and the correct information if known. Our volunteers will review and update the data.

Services

Government services

How do I get a birth certificate?

Visit the Municipal Civil Registrar (MCR) at the 1st Floor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion. Bring a valid ID and the required fee. Processing time is typically 3–5 working days. See the full requirements on the Birth Certificate service page.

How do I apply for a business permit?

Visit the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) with your DTI/SEC registration, barangay clearance, and other required documents. New permits are processed in 3–5 working days. See the Business Permit service page for full details.

How do I get a building permit?

Apply through the Municipal Engineering Office (MEO). Building permits require a zoning clearance, architectural plans, and structural plans. Processing takes 10–15 working days. See the Building Permit service page.

How do I request a barangay clearance?

Visit your barangay hall with a valid ID. Barangay clearances are typically issued the same day. This is often a prerequisite for other government transactions.

Data & Transparency

Data questions

What does "Placeholder" mean on data?

Some data is marked as "Placeholder" when the exact figure is not yet available from official sources. We use reasonable estimates based on available data and clearly mark these so residents know the difference between verified and estimated figures.

How often is the data updated?

Each data section includes a "Last updated" timestamp. Major updates happen when new COA audit reports, PSA census data, or municipal financial disclosures become available. The site is continuously improved by volunteers.

Can I download the data?

Yes. The Statistics page includes CSV download options for chart data. The Transparency page provides links to source documents. All data is publicly available and free to reuse under open data principles.

How can I verify the data myself?

Every data section includes a source attribution and, where available, a link to the original document. You can cross-reference with official sources like the COA website, PSA, or the municipal government directly.
