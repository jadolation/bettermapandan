## About Better Mapandan

**Better Mapandan** is a planned civic technology project that aims to make public information about **Mapandan, Pangasinan** easier for residents, citizens, researchers, and members of the community to discover and understand.

The project is part of the **BetterGov.ph Better LGU** community initiative, which supports independent, community-maintained digital transparency portals for Philippine local government units.

The goal is to bring important local government information together in one accessible platform, including:

* 🏛️ Local government officials and offices
* 💰 Budgets and public financial information
* 🏗️ Government projects and programs
* 📜 Ordinances and resolutions
* 📊 Public records and reports
* 📞 Government office contact information
* 🗺️ Local services and resources
* 📰 Relevant announcements and public information

> **Better Mapandan is an independent community project and is not an official website of the Municipality of Mapandan unless explicitly stated otherwise.**

---

## 🎯 Vision

To help make local government information in **Mapandan, Pangasinan** more accessible, understandable, and useful to the community.

We envision a platform where residents can easily answer questions such as:

* Who are the current local government officials?
* What projects are being implemented in our municipality?
* Where does the local government's budget go?
* What ordinances and resolutions have been passed?
* What government services are available?
* How can residents contact the appropriate government office?
* Where can citizens find publicly available government records?

---

## 🌱 Why Better Mapandan?

Government information may already be publicly available, but finding, understanding, and navigating that information can sometimes be difficult.

Better Mapandan aims to serve as a **citizen-friendly layer of access** to publicly available information.

The project does not seek to replace the official channels of the Municipality of Mapandan. Instead, it aims to make publicly available information easier to discover while encouraging transparency, civic participation, and community involvement.

## 🛠️ Planned Features

The initial portal is expected to include sections such as:

### Government Officials

A searchable and easy-to-understand directory of local government officials and relevant government offices.

### Budget & Finance

Publicly available information regarding:

* Annual budgets
* Expenditures
* Financial reports
* Appropriations
* Other relevant public financial documents

### Projects & Programs

Information about local government projects and programs, including:

* Project descriptions
* Locations
* Status
* Implementing offices
* Available public documentation

### Ordinances & Resolutions

A centralized and searchable collection of publicly available:

* Municipal ordinances
* Resolutions
* Other legislative documents

### Government Services

A directory of government services and information that can help residents determine:

* Which office handles a particular service
* Where the office is located
* How to contact the office
* What publicly available requirements or procedures apply

### Transparency & Public Records

Where legally and practically appropriate, the project will organize publicly available government records and documents into a more accessible format.

## 📚 Data Sources

The project will prioritize information that is publicly available and verifiable.

Potential sources include:

* Official Municipality of Mapandan publications and channels
* Public government records
* Commission on Audit (COA)
* Department of Budget and Management (DBM)
* Philippine government open-data resources
* Publicly available ordinances and resolutions
* Other legitimate government and civic information sources

Information should be appropriately sourced whenever possible.

---

## 🔎 Data Accuracy

Better Mapandan is intended to be a **community-maintained transparency project**.

We will make reasonable efforts to:

* Cite sources
* Verify information
* Include publication or reporting dates where available
* Distinguish current information from historical information
* Correct errors when they are identified
* Avoid presenting unverified claims as official information

If you discover inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete information, please open an issue or submit a pull request when appropriate.

## ⚖️ Independence & Transparency

Better Mapandan is a **community-led and independently maintained project**.

The project is intended to organize and present publicly available information for civic and informational purposes.

It does not claim to represent the Municipality of Mapandan, its elected officials, or any government office unless an explicit official partnership or authorization is documented.

Official government information should always be verified through the appropriate government source.

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

The project will avoid unnecessarily collecting personal information from users.

If future versions include citizen feedback, reporting, or other forms of user submissions, appropriate safeguards will be considered, including:

* Spam prevention
* Rate limiting
* CAPTCHA or equivalent protection where appropriate
* Secure handling of submitted information
* Clear privacy practices

No citizen reporting functionality will be considered ready for public use until appropriate abuse-prevention measures are implemented.
