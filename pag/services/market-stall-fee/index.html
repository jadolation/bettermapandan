Market Booth & Stall Fee Payment

Pay your market booth or stall rental fees at the Mapandan Treasury Office. Rates from ₱475 to ₱1,950 per month.

Deskripsyon

Payment of monthly rental fees for market booths and stalls at the Mapandan public market. Stall rates: ₱1,950/month (Prime), ₱1,450/month (Deluxe), ₱950/month (Standard). Booth rates: ₱975/month (Prime), ₱725/month (Deluxe), ₱475/month (Standard). New stalls: ₱2,000/month and ₱1,500/month.

Saray Kinakailangan

  • Community Tax Certificate (from Treasury Office)
  • Mayor's Permit (from Mayor's Office)

Proseso

  1. Go to Counters 5 and 8 and request computation of fees.
  2. Staff computes the stall/booth fees (15 min).
  3. Pay the required fees (3 min).
  4. Receive the Official Receipt.

Detalye na Serbisyo

Opisina ya Mangasiwa: Municipal Treasurer's Office

Klasipikasyon: Simple

Paraan: in-person

Oras na Panangproseso: 20 minutes

Bayarin: Stalls: ₱950–₱1,950/mo; Booths: ₱475–₱975/mo; New stalls: ₱1,500–₱2,000/mo

Iner a Pag-aplayan: Counters 5, 8, Municipal Treasury Office, Poblacion

Kontak: (075) 696-0831

Pinanlapuan: Mapandan Citizen's Charter

Kaukolan a Na-update: August 2025
