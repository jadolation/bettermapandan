Saray Tepet tan Verification

Inquire odino paseguroan real property saray rekord ed say Assessor's Opisina. ₱100 parad printed verification, 15 minutes.

Deskripsyon

Say Munisipal Assessor's Opisina provides impormasyon tan verification saray serbisyo regarding real property saray rekord, assessed values, buis declarations, tan arum property-related inquiries. Say client nayarian inquire nipaakar ed say estado na da property, paseguroan assessed values, usisaen parad outstanding obligations, odino man-request general impormasyon nipaakar ed property taxation. Say Assessor's Opisina staff provides say impormasyon manlapud da saray rekord.

Saray Kinakailangan

  • Valid Government-issued ID
  • Property saray detalye (lot numero, block, street, barangay) no inquiring nipaakar ed sakey ya specific property

Proseso

  1. Onla ed say Assessor's Opisina tan present sikayo duga ID.
  2. State sikayo inquiry odino verification man-request pian say staff.
  3. Staff looks up say impormasyon ed Saray rekord (10 min).
  4. Staff provides say impormasyon odino prints say verification dokumento (3 min).
  5. Manbayad ₱100 ed say Treasury Opisina no sakey ya printed dokumento et kailangan (2 min).

Detalye na Serbisyo

Opisina ya Mangasiwa: Munisipal Assessor's Opisina (MASSO)

Klasipikasyon: Simple

Paraan: in-person

Oras na Panangproseso: 15 minuto

Bayarin: ₱100

Iner a Pag-aplayan: Munisipal Assessor's Opisina, Pangunahing Munisipal Building, Poblacion

Kontak: (075) 633-2653 / 0945-114-2515

Pinanlapuan: Mapandan Citizen's Charter

Kaukolan a Na-update: August 2025
