About Mapandan

The municipality

History

Mapandan was created as an independent pueblo on December 28, 1887, through Director General No. 39. After a period of financial insolvency (1905–1908) when it was re-absorbed into Mangaldan, the town was re-established as an independent municipality in 1909 under Executive Order No. 10 by Governor General James F. Smith. The town was named for the abundance of fragrant pandan plants in the area.

Geography

Mapandan is a third-class municipality in the province of Pangasinan, Philippines. It covers a land area of 30.00 km² and is composed of 15 barangays. The municipality is located in the western part of Pangasinan, bordered by the Lingayen Gulf to the north and the Agno River floodplain to the south.

About the Project

What is Better Mapandan?

Better Mapandan is an independent civic transparency portal built and maintained by volunteers. It is part of the BetterGov.ph initiative to create open, accessible civic information websites for Philippine municipalities.

Our Mission

To make government information accessible, understandable, and actionable for every Mapandan resident. We believe that open data strengthens democracy and empowers communities to participate meaningfully in local governance.

How It Works

We compile publicly available government data — from COA audit reports, PSA census data, municipal disclosures, and provincial issuances — into a single, easy-to-navigate website. Every piece of information is sourced and attributed.

Open Source

The source code is publicly available. Developers can contribute, audit, and improve the platform.

View on GitHub →

Independent

Better Mapandan is not an official government website. It is a community-run project that operates independently of the Local Government Unit.

Volunteer-Run

Built and maintained entirely by volunteers. No government funding is used. Cost to the people of Mapandan: ₱0.
