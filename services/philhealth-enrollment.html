Description

The Population Office facilitates group enrollment of indigent residents to PhilHealth under the Local Government Unit (LGU)-sponsored program. The LGU subsidizes the premium contributions of qualified beneficiaries. Enrolled members receive health insurance coverage for hospitalization and medical services at PhilHealth-accredited facilities. The Population Office verifies eligibility, collects required documents, and submits the enrollment to PhilHealth.

Requirements

  • Valid Government-issued ID
  • Certificate of Indigency from the Barangay Captain
  • Birth Certificate or any valid ID showing date of birth

Procedure

  1. Visit the Population Office and submit the required documents.
  2. Staff verifies eligibility and reviews the documents (3 min).
  3. Staff fills out the PhilHealth enrollment form (3 min).
  4. Staff submits the enrollment to PhilHealth for validation (2 min).
  5. Receive confirmation of enrollment. PhilHealth ID will be distributed upon availability (2 min).

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Population Office

Classification: Simple

Mode: in-person

Processing Time: 10 minutes (processing); enrollment effective upon PhilHealth validation

Fee: Free (LGU-subsidized)

Where to Apply: Municipal Population Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831

Source: Mapandan Citizen's Charter
