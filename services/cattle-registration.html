Large Cattle Ownership & Brand Registration

Register or transfer large cattle ownership in Mapandan. ₱155–₱160 including documentary stamps.

Description

Issuance of Certificate of Ownership, Transfer of Ownership of Large Cattle, and Registration of Cattle Brand. Fees: ₱155 for certificate of ownership (including doc stamps), ₱160 for transfer of ownership (including doc stamps), ₱155 for registration of brand (including doc stamps). The staff enumerates distinguishing marks and physical characteristics of the cattle.

Requirements

  • Copy of the Certificate of Large Cattle
  • Steel Branding of Owner (2x2 in size)
  • Personal appearance of the owner of the registered brand

Procedure

  1. Go to Counter 2 and request for registration of ownership.
  2. Staff prepares documents (5 min).
  3. Enumerate distinguishing marks of cattle — brand, marks, and permanent physical characteristics (10 min).
  4. Pay the required fees (3 min).
  5. Receive the Certificate of Ownership/Transfer of Ownership.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Municipal Treasurer's Office

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: 20 minutes

Fee: ₱155 (ownership/brand) / ₱160 (transfer) including doc stamps

Where to Apply: Counter 2, Municipal Treasury Office, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831

Source: Mapandan Citizen's Charter
