Business Permitting & Licensing

Business Permit Renewal

Renew your business permit at the BPLO in Mapandan. Annual renewal required to maintain compliance.

Description

Businesses operating within Mapandan are required to renew their business permits annually. Renewal ensures that your business remains compliant with local regulations and avoids penalties for late filing.

Requirements

  • Filled up UNIFIED Form / Community Tax Certificate (from BPLO/Treasury Office)
  • DTI/SEC/CDA Registration (from DTI/SEC/CDA Office or Online Application)
  • BIR COR (from RDO Calasiao)
  • Barangay Clearance (System Integrated)

Procedure

  1. Fill up the UNIFIED form and submit necessary requirements for verification (10 min).
  2. BPLO encodes data into the e-BPLS System.
  3. BPLO endorses applicant to Engineering, MPDC, RHU, and BFP for required certificates (44 min).
  4. Pay fees at the Treasury Office. Fees include Mayor's Permit, Business Tax (% of Gross Sales), Plate & Sticker, Weights & Measure, Storage Fee, and Garbage Fee.
  5. Proceed to the Mayor's Office for issuance of renewed Business Permit.
  6. Claim business plate and sticker at the Treasury Office.

Service Details

Responsible Office: Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO)

Classification: Simple

Processing Time: ~1 hour (similar to new business permit process)

Fee: Mayor's Permit (₱500–₱1,000) + Business Tax (% of Gross Sales) + Business Plate & Sticker (₱250) + Weights & Measure (₱100–200) + Storage Fee (₱550) + Garbage Fee (₱800–₱1,200)

Where to Apply: 1st Floor, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831 / 0998-577-3164

Source: Mapandan Citizen’s Charter
