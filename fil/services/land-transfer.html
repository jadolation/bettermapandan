Real Property at Revenue

Paglilipat ng Ownership ng Lupa

Maglipat ng ownership ng lupa sa Mapandan sa Assessor's Office. ₱150 recording fee, 30 minuto para sa Assessor processing.

Paglalarawan

Transfer of land ownership is the legal process of transferring property rights from one owner to another. The Municipal Assessor's Office (MASSO) records the transfer and updates the Tax Declaration under the new owner's name. The client first secures a tax clearance from the Treasury Office, then submits the transfer documents to the Assessor's Office. The Assessor records the transaction, cancels the old Tax Declaration, and issues a new one. Fee: ₱150 for recording.

Mga Kinakailangan

  • Deed of Sale, Deed of Donation, or other proof of transfer
  • Tax Clearance from the Municipal Treasurer's Office
  • Certified True Copy of Tax Declaration (from Assessor's Office)
  • Valid Government-issued ID of the new owner
  • BIR Certificate of Registration (if applicable)

Prosedura

  1. Secure a Tax Clearance from the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
  2. Obtain a Certified True Copy of the Tax Declaration from the Assessor's Office.
  3. Submit all required documents to the Assessor's Office.
  4. Assessor reviews and records the transfer (15 min).
  5. Assessor cancels the old Tax Declaration and prepares the new one (10 min).
  6. Pay ₱150 recording fee at the Treasury Office.
  7. Claim the new Tax Declaration under the new owner's name (5 min).

Mga Detalye ng Serbisyo

Opisyo na May Pananagutan: Municipal Assessor's Office (MASSO)

Klasipikasyon: Complex

Paraan: in-person

Oras ng Pagproseso: 30 minutes (Assessor recording); full transfer may take 15–30 working days

Bayad: ₱150 (recording fee at Assessor's Office)

Saan Mag-aapply: Municipal Assessor's Office, Main Municipal Building, Poblacion

Contact: (075) 696-0831

Pinagmulan: Mapandan Citizen's Charter

Huling Na-update: August 2025
