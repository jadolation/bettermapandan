Services Directory

Permits, registrations, and frontline services offered by each municipal office, as published in Mapandan's Citizen's Charter.

Frontline Services

Who to see, and for what

Office Who It's For Services Offered
Office of the Municipal Mayor General public, businesses Business permits, Mayor's Clearance, affidavits, marriage solemnization
Sangguniang Bayan Constituents Legislative certifications, public consultations, financial assistance requests
Municipal Treasury Office Property owners, merchants Real property tax collection, business taxes, community tax certificates (CTC), Mayor's/Municipal Tax Ordinance Permit (MTOP), large cattle registration
Municipal Assessor's Office Real property owners Certified true copies of tax declarations, property appraisals, transfers
Municipal Engineer's Office Developers, property owners Building permits, occupancy permits, demolition permits, electrical permits
MPDC / Zoning Office Commercial applicants Locational clearances, zoning compliance certificates, land use certifications
Municipal Civil Registrar General public Registration of births, marriages, and deaths; RA 9048 name/clerical correction petitions

Sourced from Mapandan's official Citizen's Charter. Processing times and requirements are set by each office — contact them directly for current details.

Digital Government

National systems in local use

Mapandan participates in several national digital platforms that speed up identity verification and permit processing.

PhilSys

National ID system. Online Step 1 registration and local Step 2 biometric capture; PhilID/ePhilID accepted for municipal transactions.

CRS-ITP2

Civil Registry System digitizing document management and connecting the Municipal Civil Registrar to the central PSA database.

eGovPH Super App

Integrated via mapandan.gov.ph for local permit processing and digital IDs, managed by the DICT.

e-LGU Portal

Online local business permit and licensing portal for tax assessments, run by DICT/DILG.

Better Mapandan

An independent transparency portal for Mapandan, Pangasinan, built and maintained by volunteers under the BetterGov.ph BetterLGU initiative — not an official government website.
